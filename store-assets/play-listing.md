## Full description
Make your knowledge useful with SoupyLab: an AI workspace for exploring tools, preparing training data, and building focused workflows.

FIND THE RIGHT TOOL
Search the workspace by name or task, filter by subject, and save favorites. Recently opened tools help you return to your work. Connection and sign-in labels explain what each tool needs before you open it.

PREPARE TRAINING EXAMPLES OFFLINE
Write prompt-and-response pairs in the offline notebook without an account. Edit and save examples on your device, search your collection, review JSONL imports before saving, and export examples with Android's share options. Use it to prepare field notes, writing examples, support answers, or a dataset for a small language model.

EXPLORE AI AND DATASET WORKFLOWS
Open SLM Lab to explore training-data tools. Signed-in workflows include importing source material, reviewing samples, and preparing datasets. Image analysis can turn screenshots, diagrams, handwritten notes, and photos of pages into source material for training examples. Images are prepared on your device before you choose to submit them for cloud analysis.
TRY PRACTICAL STARTERS
Explore module presets for summarizing field notes, rewriting text, and classifying emails. The online demo does not require an account; usage limits apply. Sign in to work with projects, modules, and connected cloud tools. Some features require credits or a qualifying plan.

CREATE AND SHARE
Explore Image Forge in your signed-in workspace. Share or save generated images through your device's available apps, and export notebook examples as JSONL. You choose the destination whenever you share.

GET STARTED AT YOUR OWN PACE
A short, dismissible guide introduces the workspace, offline notebook, and online tools. App settings gives you access to help, feedback by email, the Google Play listing, and privacy information.

The offline notebook prepares examples; it does not train or run AI models offline. Cloud AI, account operations, and online demos require an internet connection. Availability depends on your account, plan, credits, and device. Review AI output before using it in important work.
